## 일기 (Diary)

I've been thinking a lot about affordability lately, especially when it comes to housing. Rents in my city have gone up so much in the past two years. I looked at a few apartments online today, and honestly, most of them are way out of my budget. It made me realize how hard it is for young people to find a decent place to live without spending more than half their paycheck. I ended up calling my friend Jisoo, who is going through the same struggle. We agreed that affordability has become one of the biggest concerns for people our age. Hopefully things will get better, but for now we're just trying to be smart with our money.
