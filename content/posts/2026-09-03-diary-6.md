Today I visited a science expo downtown with my friend Jimin. There were so many interesting booths, but one really caught my eye. A company was showing off a rehabilitation robot. It's a pretty tall robot, and it's for people who have strokes. I had no idea technology like this existed already. It made me think about how science can genuinely change people's lives for the better.
